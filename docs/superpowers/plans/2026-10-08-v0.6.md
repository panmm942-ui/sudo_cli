## Review focus

- No budget request bypass through native retry, direct Responses or detached workers.
- Undo refuses intervening edits, symlinks and out-of-project paths.
- Web Off cannot become unrestricted through an approval or full-access mode.
- Offline chats do not trigger configuration or paid model requests.
- Voice transcripts remain literal prompts and never approvals/commands.
- Provider status uncertainty, estimates and unavailable hardware are accurately labelled.
- Scheduled work never replays completed side effects after restart.
- Saved secrets, updates, browser actions and project memory remain explicitly bounded.
